Social media management in Barcelona

A constant presence, without demands on your time.

See all services › Request a proposal

The problem with social media is rarely a lack of ideas. It is consistency. Most accounts start well, publish for three weeks and then stop, because the business takes up the time.

We take that part off your hands. We prepare the strategy, the calendar and the content, we publish and reply, and every month you receive a report on what worked and what did not.

We work from Barcelona and meet regularly, so that the content looks like your business rather than a generic account for your sector.

What it includes

Strategy and calendar

We define what to say, how often and on which platform. The calendar is approved before anything is published.

Original content

Design of the pieces and writing of the copy, in your brand identity. We can also adapt material you already have.

Publishing and community

We publish on the agreed dates and answer comments and messages in the tone we have set together.

Monthly report

A clear report, without jargon: what was published, what reach it had, what performed and what we propose for the next month.

How we work

  1. Initial audit

    We review your profiles, those of your competitors and which content has worked so far.

  2. Monthly plan

    We present the calendar with topics and dates. You approve it or ask for changes before anything is produced.

  3. Production and publishing

    We create the pieces, schedule them and handle the daily comments and messages.

  4. Report and adjustment

    At the end of the month we review the figures together and adjust the following plan with what we learned.

Who it is for

  • Businesses with open profiles that sit idle for lack of time.
  • Companies publishing without a plan that want a more consistent, more serious image.
  • Brands whose clients check them online before buying.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms do you manage?

Those that make sense for your business, normally Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Two platforms handled well beat five handled halfway.

Who takes the photos and videos?

We can work with material you already have, produce the design pieces ourselves, or arrange a photo session when the project calls for it.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. We work month to month. If at any point it stops being worthwhile, you can leave without penalty.

How long before results show?

Early signals such as reach and engagement appear within the first weeks. A solid community and clients arriving through social media take months, not days.

Do I have to approve every post?

That is your call. Some clients approve the monthly calendar and nothing else; others prefer to review each piece. Both work.
