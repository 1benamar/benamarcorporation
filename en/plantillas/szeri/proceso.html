Process

Know what to expect, step by step.

Working with an architect doesn't have to be a mystery. Here's how your project moves forward with us, from start to finish.

01
1 week

Initial consultation

We listen to what you need, visit the space, and give you an honest first assessment — even if that means telling you your idea needs adjusting.

02
2-3 weeks

Preliminary design

First design proposal with plans, massing and a ballpark budget. We refine it until it's exactly what you're looking for.

03
4-8 weeks

Executive project and permits

Complete technical documentation and management of every permit required from the local council.

04
Depends on the project

Construction

Weekly on-site follow-up so the build stays faithful to the project, with clear progress reporting.

05

Handover

Final walkthrough with you and delivery of all documentation (as-built, warranties, manuals).

Before you write to us

Frequently asked questions

How much does an architecture project cost?

It depends on the scope; after the initial consultation we give you a ballpark budget with no obligation.

How long does the whole process take?

From the initial consultation to handover, a full renovation usually takes 4 to 8 months; a new build, longer.

Do you handle the permits yourselves?

Yes, permit management is included in the executive project.

Can I start with just an idea, no plans?

Yes — in fact, that's the norm. That's exactly what the initial consultation is for.
