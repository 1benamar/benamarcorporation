Overview

Széri Design

Széri Design is one of the most recognised architecture studios in Catalonia, having delivered more than 80 projects since 2013. Born as a residential design studio, Széri has now taken the step into developing its own projects, applying the same design-led criteria that has defined every commission: spaces built for how people actually live, not for a catalogue photo.

For the investor looking for something different, Széri represents an alternative to generic development: projects located in privileged spots along the Barcelona coast, built around an "integrated community" philosophy — residential towers and villas, private green areas and local retail, all designed as a single coherent whole. With a small team and full involvement at every stage, every investment is backed by the very studio that signs off on the project — no middlemen.
