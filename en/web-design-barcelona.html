A company website is where a client checks whether you are worth calling. This service covers design, development, copy and launch, with the domain and the code in your name from day one.

For most businesses, the website is the first place a client checks before deciding whether the call is worth making. If it loads slowly, looks wrong on a phone or fails to explain what the company does, that call never happens.

At BENAMAR we design and develop custom corporate websites from Barcelona. No bought templates and no features nobody will use: only what the business needs to explain itself well and turn visits into customers.

The result is a website of your own, fast on mobile and ready from day one for Google to understand it. And it stays in your name, with the domain, the hosting and the code under your control.

What it includes

What the service covers, with no small print.

Custom design

Lightweight development

Our own code, without heavy page builders. The site loads quickly on mobile, which is where most of the traffic comes from.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we get before starting, answered plainly.

How long does a corporate website take?

It depends on the number of pages and on how quickly we receive copy and photographs. A presentation website is usually live between two and four weeks after we start.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted separately, because not all websites need the same work. You get a fixed price before we begin, and that price does not change unless you add new work.

Does the website belong to me?

Yes. The domain, the hosting and the code are yours. If one day you decide to work with another company, you take everything with you.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. We prepare the parts that change often so that you can edit them without touching code, and we show you how.

Do you only work in Barcelona?

No. We are based in Barcelona and meet clients in person here and nearby, but the work itself runs just as well remotely. We serve clients across Spain and abroad, in Spanish, English and French.
