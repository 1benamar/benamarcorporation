Web design

A website that conveys the real value of your business.

See all services › Request a proposal

For most businesses, the website is the first place a client checks before deciding whether the call is worth making. If it loads slowly, looks wrong on a phone or fails to explain what the company does, that call never happens.

At BENAMAR we design and develop custom corporate websites from Barcelona. No bought templates and no features nobody will use: only what the business needs to explain itself well and turn visits into customers.

The result is a website of your own, fast on mobile and ready from day one for Google to understand it. And it stays in your name, with the domain, the hosting and the code under your control.

What it includes

Custom design

Every website starts from scratch, built around the identity of the business. We do not adapt generic templates or reuse the same layout in a different colour.

Lightweight development

Our own code, without heavy page builders. The site loads quickly on mobile, which is where most of the traffic comes from.

Structure and copy

We order the information so that a visitor understands within seconds what the business offers and what to do next.

Ready to rank

Tags, speed, language versions and structured data in place from the first day, so that Google can read the site properly.

How we work

  1. First conversation

    We learn about the business, who it serves and what the website has to achieve. No automated questionnaires.

  2. Fixed proposal

    Budget and deadline in writing before we start, with the scope set out in detail. No open-ended items.

  3. Design and development

    You review the work at each stage and ask for changes before we move on. Nothing is signed off without you seeing it.

  4. Launch and follow-up

    We leave the site published, with the domain and email configured, and measured so you know what visitors do.

Who it is for

  • Established businesses whose website has fallen behind or never delivered results.
  • Professionals and new companies that need a serious first website, not an improvised page.
  • Companies working in several languages that need a properly written version of each.
  • Companies outside Barcelona, in Spain or abroad, that prefer to work remotely.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a corporate website take?

It depends on the number of pages and on how quickly we receive copy and photographs. A presentation website is usually live between three and six weeks after we start.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted separately, because not all websites need the same work. You get a fixed price before we begin, and that price does not change unless you add new work.

Does the website belong to me?

Yes. The domain, the hosting and the code are yours. If one day you decide to work with another company, you take everything with you.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. We prepare the parts that change often so that you can edit them without touching code, and we show you how.

Do you only work in Barcelona?

No. We are based in Barcelona and meet clients in person here and nearby, but the work itself runs just as well remotely. We serve clients across Spain and abroad, in Spanish, English and French.

Tell us what your business needs. We reply within 24 hours with a concrete proposal.
